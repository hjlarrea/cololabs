I'm an engineer and technical product manager with a builder's mindset, a strategic perspective, and a passion for solving complex problems through technology.

My experience spans both engineering and product management, two disciplines I see not as separate career paths, but as complementary ways of approaching problems. Engineering allows me to understand how things work, challenge technical assumptions, and build reliable solutions. Product thinking helps me understand why those solutions matter, identify opportunities, and focus on creating meaningful value.

Throughout my career, I've moved between hands-on engineering and product leadership, often operating somewhere in between. I enjoy both the technical depth of designing and building systems and the broader challenge of defining direction, balancing trade-offs, and turning ideas into reality.

Curiosity has always been a driving force behind what I do. I'm constantly experimenting with technologies, exploring ideas, and building things simply for the joy of learning. I believe the best way to understand something is to get your hands dirty, challenge assumptions, and figure out how it works.

Above all, I believe technology is a means, not an end. What matters most isn't the complexity of what we build, but the problems we solve and the value we create along the way.

First and foremost, I'm a father, a role I absolutely love and one that brings endless joy, challenges, and new perspectives to my life.

I enjoy pushing my limits through demanding workouts, watching movies, listening to music, and reading. I'm naturally curious and always looking for something new to learn. With AI, I've found an incredible force multiplier for that curiosity, allowing me to explore ideas, experiment, and build things in ways I could only imagine before.

# What is ColoLabs?
ColoLabs is a creative umbrella and personal workspace where curiosity turns into output. It is a place for experimenting across formats: building tools and apps, creating AI-assisted content and short videos, exploring technical domains such as IT infrastructure, and writing down ideas worth developing. The site is intentionally flexible: a playground for iteration, learning, and expression where ideas can be tested, shaped, and sometimes turned into something real.

# Responsible AI Use Policy
I embrace AI as a powerful tool to accelerate learning, creativity, and problem-solving. I use it to explore ideas, challenge assumptions, and improve the way I work. However, I believe that **AI should augment human judgment, not replace it**. I take responsibility for everything I create and publish, applying critical thinking, fact-checking, and appropriate review to ensure accuracy, quality, and safety. I also strive to use AI responsibly, respecting privacy, intellectual property, and the limitations of the technology. Ultimately, AI may help me get there faster, but the decisions, accountability, and final responsibility remain mine.
